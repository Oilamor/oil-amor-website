## 1. How orders consume crystals

- Every bottled oil order ships with crystal chips matched to that oil's pairing(s) (see §2).
- Custom blends (Mixing Atelier) ship with the customer's chosen crystals from the 22-crystal atelier list.
- **Refills ship no crystals** (forever bottles retain theirs).
- Chips per bottle size (uniform across all oils):

| Bottle size | Chips | Total weight |
|---|---|---|
| 5ml | 2 | 8 g |
| 10ml | 4 | 15 g |
| 15ml | 6 | 25 g |
| 20ml | 8 | 35 g |
| 30ml | 12 | 50 g |

Average ≈ **4 g per chip** — use that to convert chip counts to kilos when buying.
